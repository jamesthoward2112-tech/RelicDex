The story

Familiar places. A new mystery.

Start in New Bark Town with a Pikachu entrusted to you by Professor Elm. The discovery of ancient fossils and long-forgotten evolutions reshapes the classic Johto adventure while preserving the characters, routes and story beats you remember.

Features

The Johto adventure, evolved.

PRU is not just a reskin. New Pokémon, progression balancing, recurring rivals and modernized conveniences build on the two-region GBA adventure.

✦

Ancient evolutions

At level 45+, an Ancient Stone unlocks many custom evolution branches. Eeveelutions and special cases retain their own methods.

◇

444-species roster

Pokémon from Kanto, Johto and Hoenn alongside approved Relic and custom additions. The online RelicDex provides the reference list.

⚔

Rebalanced Johto

Gym Leaders, route trainers and wild encounters have been retuned around the journey and its expanding roster.

♟

Recurring rivals

Meet Steevee and Neevee on Route 37, then face the Eevee Twins again later in Johto and Kanto.

❖

Useful training tools

Move-learning improvements, Chansey training encounters and named TMs help your team keep pace.

◈

Custom artwork

Approved PRL/PRU sprites, title artwork and signature encounters give Relic Pokémon a distinct look.

Development note: Some features are source/build-verified but still awaiting full playthrough testing. Planned Kanto roaming birds and further late-game encounter tuning are not presented as finished.

Questions

Before you start.

What is Pokémon Relics Ultimate?

A fan-made Pokémon GBA project with a Johto-first campaign, continuation into Kanto, an expanded canonical RelicDex and bespoke Relic evolutions.

Where do I see every Pokémon and evolution method?

Use RelicDex, which contains the 444-entry species list, artwork and evolution information. For development-specific discrepancies, consult the current update log.

Is the game finished and fully tested?

No. Builds and source changes have passed automated checks, but individual gameplay scenarios, catchability and completion remain under testing. Do not mistake source integration for a final release.

Where is the latest ROM or patch?

Check the PRU GitHub Actions builds for development artifacts and the Releases page for any publicly posted release. Back up your save before testing a new build.
